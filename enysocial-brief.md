# eny.social Landing Page Brief

## What is eny.social?

A decentralized social media platform built on the AT Protocol (same protocol as Bluesky). It targets normal people (not developers). It combines social networking with local city services, starting in Offenbach, Germany. Built and hosted in Europe.

## Stack

Next.js

## Design Direction

Warm, organic, human. Not techy or startup-generic. The design uses:

- **Typography**: Large editorial serif for headlines, clean sans-serif for body text. Bold, expressive type hierarchy.
- **Color palette**: Warm coral/salmon (#E8735A-ish), teal/cyan accents, warm neutrals, soft pinks. Creamy off-white background.
- **Organic blob shapes**: Freeform coral and teal blobs as background elements throughout the page. Not geometric — soft, painterly, irregular.
- **Photo treatments**: Circular and rounded-rectangle crops of diverse, warm, candid photos of real people. Some photos overlap with the blob shapes.
- **Decorative lines**: Thin hand-drawn-feeling curved lines connecting sections.
- **Overall feel**: Editorial, European, inviting. Like a magazine layout meets a community board.

## Design Notes

- Decorative organic blob shapes (coral and teal) appear throughout as background elements — they bleed between sections and create visual continuity
- Thin curved decorative lines (hand-drawn feeling) weave through the page connecting sections
- Photos are always circular or rounded-rectangle cropped, never sharp rectangles
- Labels/categories use a pill/badge style with accent color background
- The page has a flowing, organic rhythm — not rigid grid-based sections
- Animations should be smooth: gentle scroll reveals, subtle float on avatars, text cycling in hero
- Responsive design needed
- Language: English for now
- This is a pre-launch waitlist page. The platform is being built.
